# Pre-Publish Review Checklist — FactoryLM Video Content

**FAC-19 Deliverable**
**Time budget:** 5 minutes human review max. Everything else is automated.

---

## Technical Gates (auto-checked by `shorts_pipeline.py`)

These are hard gates. Pipeline rejects the file if any fail.

- [ ] **Duration:** 58–60s (Shorts hard cap — rejected if outside range)
- [ ] **Resolution:** 1080×1920 (9:16 vertical)
- [ ] **No black bars** — vertical crop confirmed (smart crop, not letterbox)
- [ ] **Audio:** −14 LUFS normalized, no clipping above −1 dBTP
- [ ] **Hook card:** Bold text overlay appears in first 3 seconds
- [ ] **End card:** CTA visible in final 10 seconds
- [ ] **Watermark:** "factorylm.com" present, bottom-right, semi-transparent
- [ ] **Captions:** Burned-in (Whisper transcription, bold_industrial style)

## Content Review (5-minute human gate)

### Hook
- [ ] **Hook works with sound OFF** — text or visual tells the story without audio
- [ ] **Hook works with sound ON** — alarm, demo audio, or voice adds urgency
- [ ] Hook is in the **first 3 seconds** — no slow intro, no logo reveal

### Claim & Proof
- [ ] **Every claim is provable** — real hardware, not simulation or mockup
- [ ] **Outsider engineer test** — a competent engineer unfamiliar with FactoryLM understands the value
  - Trust the ICP: say "VFD overcurrent at 87Hz", not "the motor thing"
  - They know what E005 means. Explain why MIRA's answer is better than theirs.
  - Do NOT dumb it down. Dumbing down signals you don't trust your audience.
- [ ] **No unverifiable stats** — if it says "<2 seconds", the recording must show it

### CTA
- [ ] **Exactly ONE call to action** — not "like, subscribe, AND visit AND comment"
- [ ] CTA is spoken AND shown as text
- [ ] CTA URL = `factorylm.com` (landing page) OR `calendly.com/mike-cranesync/30min` (Week 4 only)

### Brand Safety
- [ ] **No competitor names spelled out** — say "enterprise solutions" not "Augury"
- [ ] No unintentional background content (whiteboards, email on screen, personal info)
- [ ] FactoryLM watermark present and readable

### Thumbnail
- [ ] **Text ≤ 5 words** — readable at 100px width (mobile thumbnail size)
- [ ] Series color matches thumbnail background (see series color map)
- [ ] No faces cropped at edges
- [ ] High contrast — readable on both light and dark backgrounds

## Series-Specific Rules

### Before It Breaks
- Must show fault → diagnosis → resolution arc in 60s
- "Before It Breaks" means the fault is caught BEFORE failure — never show a destroyed machine as proof

### $500K vs $30
- Always show the $30 FactoryLM result, not just the competitor's price
- Never show a competitor's logo. Describe the category ("enterprise predictive maintenance platforms")

### Live Diagnosis
- Must be uncut — no editing that hides latency or manual steps
- Show the real Telegram conversation, not a recreated mockup

### Tech Stories
- Face-cam required — no face = no "Tech Stories" series
- One story arc per video: problem → build → result. No meandering.

### Off the Grid
- Must physically demonstrate air-gap — show ethernet unplugged, WiFi disabled
- Never claim "100% offline" unless the demo machine has no network adapters active

## Reject Conditions (send back to production)

Reject if ANY of the following are true:

| Condition | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| Duration outside 58–60s | YouTube algorithm penalty for Shorts |
| Black bars visible | Amateur production signal |
| Claim unsupported by footage | Credibility risk |
| Multiple CTAs in one video | Conversion rate drops with split attention |
| Competitor name spoken or shown | Legal and brand risk |
| Thumbnail text > 5 words | Unreadable on mobile |
| No caption burn-in | 85% of social video watched muted |
| Audio clipping | Immediate credibility loss |
