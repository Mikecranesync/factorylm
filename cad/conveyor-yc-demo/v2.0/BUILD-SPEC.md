## Actual Construction (From Photos)

### Frame Material
- **2x4 lumber** (not Unistrut) - simpler, cheaper, faster
- Natural wood finish (unpainted)

### Roller Assembly (OBSERVED)
- **PVC pipe** (~2" diameter, Schedule 40 markings visible)
- **Wood dowel shaft** (~3/4" or 1") through center
- Dowel extends past PVC on both ends for mounting

### Frame Joints
- **Butt joints** with deck screws (black oxide, ~3")
- **No pre-drill** visible - direct drive
- Legs attached flush to outside of frame

### Current Dimensions (Estimated from photo)
- Frame width: ~14-16" (inside)
- Leg height: ~30-36"
- PVC roller length: ~14" (fits inside frame)

## Assembly Sequence (Actual)

1. ✅ Cut 2x4s to length
2. ✅ Assemble rectangular frame
3. ✅ Attach legs
4. ✅ Insert roller (PVC on dowel)
5. ⏳ Add second roller
6. ⏳ Mount motor
7. ⏳ Add belt
8. ⏳ Wire VFD
9. ⏳ Test!
